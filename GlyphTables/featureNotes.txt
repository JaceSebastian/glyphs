-Base Glyph (Last 3 features)
    - Idea is that a lot of words are an action/movement and a metaphorical extension, ie invoke = call/shout + spellcasting 
        so one can guess the meaning of a lot of glyphs, yet there is enough variety to have unique verbs as needed.
        ie root die has +harm=murder, +oath=release oath, +service=kill, assassinate, +existance=end, +speech=conclude
        - follow+oath is following clauses in bargain, not physically following, which helps with demon's trying to misinterpret
